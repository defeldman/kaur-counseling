About Sohavani Mand

Kaur Counseling — “Kaur” is pronounced like “Core”

Therapy rooted in authenticity, curiosity, and connection.

Hi, and welcome — I'm glad you're here. If you've found your way to this page, something is asking for attention.

Maybe you're deeply self-aware but still repeating the same patterns. Maybe you're holding everything together with duct tape and caffeine, patching one day onto the next. Maybe you don't recognize yourself anymore, or you're just tired — tired of pushing through, tired of not knowing what comes next.

If you're feeling stuck, unmoored, or ready for something to change, you've found a safe space to begin. Together we'll make sense of what's brought you here — how your experiences, relationships, culture, and coping patterns have shaped the way you move through the world. My approach is warm, collaborative, trauma-informed, and rooted in authenticity. I'll offer compassion, and I'll also gently challenge the patterns that aren't serving you anymore.

Over time, therapy can help you feel more grounded, more connected to yourself and others, and most importantly, like you again. Duct tape and caffeine can only do so much. I'm ready to help you trade the quick fixes for something that actually lasts.

People we serve

Who this room is for.

Whoever you are, however you got here, this is a space held with care for the whole of you.

Women

Therapy can be a space to figure yourself out by exploring some of life's bigger questions: What do you want? What matters to you? And what have you been told you're supposed to want? It's a space to talk about how exhausting it can be to feel like you're always falling short, and to slow down enough to get curious about what actually feels true for you.

People of Color

Therapy can be a space to explore how culture, family, identity, and the expectations you've grown up with have shaped the way you see yourself and your choices. It's a place to talk about the experience of moving between different worlds, carrying family expectations, or feeling caught between who you are and who others expect you to be. A space to make sense of those experiences without having to choose between the different parts of yourself.

Men

Therapy can be a place to explore the parts of yourself that can be harder to talk about: shame, vulnerability, relationships, communication, and the pressure to always have it together. We can look at the expectations you hold for yourself, how they shape the way you move through the world, and make room for the more sensitive parts of who you are. A place to meet yourself where you are, take accountability without shame, and build a more compassionate relationship with yourself.

Teens Coming of Age

Adolescence is its own country. A quiet, judgment-free space for teens to untangle the pressure, the noise, and the big feelings.

LGBTQ+ Community

A space to speak openly about identity, relationships, and navigating a world that hasn't always made room. Wherever you are on the journey, you're welcome here.

Modalities

Three lenses, one gentle practice.

No single approach fits every life. These are the frameworks I draw from, woven together to meet your particular story.

◯⌕
01

Relational Therapy

The relationship is the therapy. We slow down the space between you and another: the ruptures, the rhythms. And we learn how connection can be repaired and reimagined.

♡
02

Attachment

Early bonds leave a quiet blueprint. Together we trace those patterns with care, making the invisible legible so that security can grow where uncertainty once lived.

◉
03

Internal Family Systems

Inside you is a whole constellation of parts — protectors, exiles, the calm core beneath. We welcome each voice, gently, and help them find their way home to one another.

Helping you understand yourself — not just your symptoms.
