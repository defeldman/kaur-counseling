[H2] A superbill for reimbursement
[P] I'm out-of-network with insurance plans, but I can provide a superbill after each session. You submit it to your insurance company for possible reimbursement.
[P] Many clients receive 50–80% of the session fee back, depending on their plan. Call the number on your insurance card to ask about out-of-network mental health benefits, deductibles, and reimbursement rates.
[H2] Why pay out of pocket?
[P] Choosing not to bill insurance means your care stays private and your treatment is shaped by what you need, not by a diagnosis or a limit set by your plan. It's also a way to work at the pace and depth that is right for you.
