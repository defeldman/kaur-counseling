[SPAN] PEOPLE WE SERVE
[H3] Who this room is for.
[P] Whoever you are, however you got here, this is a space held with care for the whole of you.
[H3] Women
[P] Therapy can be a space to figure yourself out by exploring some of life's bigger questions: What do you want? What matters to you? And what have you been told you're supposed to want? It's a space to talk about how exhausting it can be to feel like you're always falling short, and to slow down enough to get curious about what actually feels true for you.
[H3] People of Color
[P] Therapy can be a space to explore how culture, family, identity, and the expectations you've grown up with have shaped the way you see yourself and your choices. It's a place to talk about the experience of moving between different worlds, carrying family expectations, or feeling caught between who you are and who others expect you to be. A space to make sense of those experiences without having to choose between the different parts of yourself.
[H3] Men
[P] Therapy can be a place to explore the parts of yourself that can be harder to talk about: shame, vulnerability, relationships, communication, and the pressure to always have it together. We can look at the expectations you hold for yourself, how they shape the way you move through the world, and make room for the more sensitive parts of who you are. A place to meet yourself where you are, take accountability without shame, and build a more compassionate relationship with yourself.
[H3] Teens Coming of Age
[P] Adolescence is its own country. A quiet, judgment-free space for teens to untangle the pressure, the noise, and the big feelings.
[H3] LGBTQ+ Community
