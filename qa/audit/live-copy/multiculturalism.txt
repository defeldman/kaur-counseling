[H1] Multicultural & Cross-Cultural Therapy
[P] When your identity doesn't fit neatly into one box.
[P] To live between cultures is to hold more than one home inside you: its gifts and its grief, its belonging and its exile. My work is to make room for the whole of you: the language you dream in, the values you've chosen, and the ones still being negotiated. You do not have to compress yourself to be understood here.
[H2] Multicultural Therapy
[P] We explore how your race, ethnicity, and cultural background have shaped the way you see yourself and the world, making room for every layer of your identity rather than asking you to choose just one.
[H2] Acculturation & Assimilation Stress
[P] We tend to the strain of holding your heritage culture alongside a dominant one that doesn't always make space for it: the codeswitching, the guilt, the quiet exhaustion, so you can move between worlds without losing yourself.
[H2] First-Generation & Second-Generation Issues
[P] We unpack the family pressures, guilt, and identity conflicts that come with being a child of immigrants: the expectations you carry, the roles you play, and the version of yourself you're finally allowed to become.
[H2] Third Culture Kid (TCK) Therapy
[P] We make sense of a belonging that never felt simple, raised in a culture that wasn't your parents' and maybe wasn't your passport's, so the question of 'where are you from?' stops feeling like a small crisis.
[H2] Intergenerational Trauma Therapy
[P] We gently trace the trauma, expectations, and communication gaps passed down from your parents, not to assign blame, but to understand what was inherited and choose what you want to carry forward.
