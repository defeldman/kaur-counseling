[H1] Anxiety & Depression.
[P] Anxiety and depression can look completely different, but both have a way of adding a layer to everyday life that can make everything feel harder than it should.
[H2] Anxiety
[P] Anxiety can keep your mind running long after you want it to stop. It can make decisions feel overwhelming, turn small things into big things, and leave you constantly anticipating what might go wrong.
[H2] Depression
[P] Depression can make everything feel heavier in a different way. Things that once felt meaningful can feel distant, motivation can disappear, and even basic tasks can take more effort than you have to give.
[H2] When they show up together
[P] Sometimes they show up together. Sometimes one takes over for a while and the other follows. And sometimes it's hard to explain what's happening at all — you just know that life feels harder than it used to. You don't have to figure it out alone.
[H2] How we work with it
[P] I'm not here to tell you to "just think positively" or hand you a list of things you should be doing differently. I'm here to meet you where you are, understand what you're carrying, and work through it with you — one piece at a time.
[DIV] Understand what's happening beneath the surface
[DIV] Recognize the patterns that keep you stuck
[DIV] Respond to overwhelm without shutting down
[DIV] Challenge thoughts that aren't serving you
[DIV] Reconnect with the things that matter to you
[DIV] Make changes that feel realistic, not overwhelming
