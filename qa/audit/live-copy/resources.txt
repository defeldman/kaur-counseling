[SPAN] A POEM TO SIT WITH
[H2] The Guest House
[P] This being human is a guest house.
[P] Every morning a new arrival.
[P] A joy, a depression, a meanness,
[P] some momentary awareness comes
[P] as an unexpected visitor.
[P] Welcome and entertain them all!
[P] Even if they're a crowd of sorrows,
[P] who violently sweep your house
[P] empty of its furniture,
[P] still, treat each guest honorably.
[P] He may be clearing you out
[P] for some new delight.
[P] The dark thought, the shame, the malice,
[P] meet them at the door laughing,
[P] and invite them in.
[P] Be grateful for whoever comes,
[P] because each has been sent
[P] as a guide from beyond.
[FIGCAPTION] JALALUDDIN RUMI
[P] Translated by Coleman Barks · Scottish Poetry Library
